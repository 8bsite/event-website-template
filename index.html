One hall, four heats

Pier Arena opens wall to wall. Four zones run side by side all day, so you can leave the concert queue for tteokbokki and still make the chorus.

  1. Main stage

    12:00 – 23:00

    The headliners at night, the cover stage by day. A standing floor for 6,000, a raised deck at the back for anyone who would rather see than be seen.

  2. Battle floor

    14:30 – 18:30

    A sprung dance floor ringed by bleachers. Open workshops in the morning, the crew battle from 14:30, random play dance for everyone at six.

  3. Beauty lab

    12:30 – 20:00

    Twelve chairs, Korean skincare brands and makeup artists working on the looks from this year's videos. Walk in or book a slot at the gate.

  4. Food street

    12:00 – 23:30

    Twenty stalls along the river wall: tteokbokki, corn dogs, hotteok, fried chicken and bingsu, plus a tea bar that stays quiet when the hall does not.

Before you come

Still unsure about something? The ticket desk answers within a day.

hello@ondo.example
Is there an age limit?

All ages are welcome. Under-16s need an adult with them on the standing floor, and under-12s go free with a paying adult.

Can I bring my lightstick?

Yes: official lightsticks, banners up to A3 and one small bag. No poles, chairs or cameras with detachable lenses.

Do I need cash?

No. Food street and the merch stand take cards and phones only, and there is a top-up point by the gate for prepaid cards.

Is the hall accessible?

Step-free from the tram stop to every zone, a raised viewing deck by the main stage and a quiet room next to the beauty lab. Companions go free.

What if a set time changes?

The schedule on this page and in the app updates live, and we send a push note an hour before any change on the main stage.

Can I get a refund?

Up to 30 days before the festival, minus a €5 fee. After that you can resell your pass at face value through our official exchange.
